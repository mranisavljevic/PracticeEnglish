# Implementation Plan: Volume 4 Student Book & "Coming Soon" Placeholders

**Branch**: `006-vol4-and-coming-soon` | **Date**: 2026-11-12 | **Spec**: [spec.md](./spec.md)

**Input**: Feature specification from `/specs/006-vol4-and-coming-soon/spec.md`

## Summary

Make the Level 4 (Volume 4) Student Book live by filling its track ranges into `docs/UNITS-DRAFT.md`, and show five greyed-out "coming soon" tiles (Vol 1 SB/WB, Vol 3 SB/WB, Vol 4 WB). The catalogue generator keeps one rule: a book with track ranges is live, a book without them is a placeholder. Placeholders are emitted into a new top-level `comingSoon` array in `catalog.json`, separate from `books`. Because every existing lookup (resume, scan, analytics, unit/track routes) reads `books`, placeholders cannot leak into them. Releasing a placeholder later is just filling its column in `UNITS-DRAFT.md` and rebuilding.

## Deviations from the spec (need owner awareness)

1. **Unit 6 is empty.** Local Vol 4 SB audio has tracks 1–27 and 35–84; tracks 28–34 (chapter 6) were never published (see `docs/AUDIO-GAPS-AND-DUPLICATES.txt`, `docs/TODO.md`). FR-001 says 14 units, but a unit with no tracks is a dead end. The generator omits empty units, so the book shows **13 units** (unit 6 missing) until the audio is found, then reappears on rebuild. If the owner prefers a visible but empty "Unit 6", that is a small change in the generator.
2. **Unit titles.** The spec assumes "Unit N" with no titles, but `docs/UNITS-DRAFT.md` already lists Level 4 chapter titles (Education, Colonial Times, …). The generator already reads them for Vol 2, so Vol 4 will show "Unit N: Title" for consistency. Reversible by blanking the title cells.
